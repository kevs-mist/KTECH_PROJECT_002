
KTECH.Field Service CRMPRODUCT OVERVIEW&amp; FEATURE GUIDEPREPARED FOR ATM Services CompanyPREPARED BY Ktech — Keval TechnicalsDATE April 1, 2026  /  Version 1.0STATUS Ready for Review ↗AHMEDABAD, GUJARAT, INDIA  — 01 — WHAT IS THISKTECH Field CRM.KTECH Field CRM is a web-based application built specifically for your ATM maintenance and field operations team. It replaces the current manual process of managing field engineers through phone calls, WhatsApp messages, and spreadsheets with a single, organised digital system that everyone can use.The system works on desktop computers and mobile phones. Field engineers use it on their phones while on the go, and operations admins manage everything from a central dashboard.IN SIMPLE TERMSWhen an ATM has a problem, an admin creates a ticket, assigns it to an available engineer, the engineer visits the ATM and fixes the issue, uploads a photo or video as proof, and closes the ticket — all tracked in one place, in real time.— 02 — WHY IT EXISTSProblems This System Solves.Today, ATM service operations face recurring challenges that cost time, create confusion, and leave no clear record of work done. KTECH Field CRM is designed to solve each of these.PROBLEM TODAYHOW KTECH FIELD CRM FIXES ITNo single place to track ATM issuesEvery issue is logged as a ticket — nothing gets lostEngineers don’t know where to goEach ticket has the ATM location with a one-tap link to Google MapsAdmin can’t see what engineers are doingLive online / offline status and assignments always visibleBanks can’t confirm if the issue was fixedEngineers upload photo or video proof before closing any ticketNo history of past workEvery ticket keeps a full log of every status change and actionVerbal comms lead to missed workAll assignments happen inside the app — nothing via WhatsApp or calls— 03 — USER ROLESWho Uses the System.KTECH Field CRM is designed for three types of users. Each user sees only what is relevant to their role — nothing more.Operations AdminThe admin is the central controller of the system. They log in from a desktop or mobile, have full visibility of all tickets and all engineers, and can create, assign, and manage every ticket in the system.Field EngineerThe engineer logs in on their mobile phone. They can see tickets assigned to them, navigate to ATM locations via Google Maps, and close tickets after completing the work. When they log in, their status automatically shows as Online.Bank Authority — Phase 2In a future phase, bank representatives can view tickets for their branch, see proof of work, confirm an issue is resolved, and re-raise it if the problem persists.— 04 — ADMIN FEATURESAdmin — Full Feature Detail.The admin has access to three main sections: Dashboard, Tickets, and Engineers.01 / DashboardThe Dashboard is the first thing an admin sees after logging in. It gives a real-time summary of the entire operation at a glance.METRICWHAT IT MEANSTotal TicketsEvery ATM issue ever logged in the systemOpen TicketsTickets currently active and not yet resolvedClosed TicketsTickets that have been successfully resolvedATMs in ServiceTotal number of ATMs managed by your companyThe dashboard updates in real time — any change made anywhere in the system appears immediately without needing to refresh the page.02 / TicketsThe Tickets section shows every ticket in the system. Each entry in the list shows the ticket number, title, current status, ATM ID, and the date it was created. Admins can filter by All, Open, or Closed, and search by ATM ID or Bank ID.Creating a New TicketTo raise a new issue, the admin fills out a short form:A title and description of the issueThe ATM ID and Bank ID it relates toThe type of issue — from a dropdown (card reader failure, network issue, power failure, and more)The ATM location and Bank location, used by the engineer for navigationAn optional direct assignment to a specific engineerManaging Existing TicketsFrom any ticket detail, the admin can view the full issue description, see the engineer’s resolution notes and proof media once closed, and manually close any open ticket if required.03 / EngineersThe Engineers section gives the admin a live view of every engineer, grouped by their current status.WHAT YOU SEEDETAILOnline / Offline badgeGreen = engineer is currently logged in and activeActive ticketsHow many tickets this engineer is currently working onTickets closed (all time)A running total of all work completed by the engineerAssign ticketAdmin can assign any open ticket to an engineer directly from their profile— 05 — ENGINEER FEATURESEngineer — Full Feature Detail.The engineer’s experience is designed entirely for mobile phones. The interface uses large buttons and clear labels — no technical knowledge is required.01 / Logging InThe engineer logs in with an email address and password. The moment they log in, their status is automatically set to Online — visible to the admin in real time. When they log out or close the app, their status switches to Offline automatically.02 / Engineer DashboardAfter logging in, the engineer sees three things:Total number of tickets in the system (for context)Tickets assigned specifically to themUnassigned tickets that they can choose to take on03 / Ticket Detail ViewTapping any ticket opens the full detail view, which shows:Ticket number and a full description of the issueATM ID and Bank IDATM location with a one-tap “Open in Google Maps” buttonBank location for referenceGOOGLE MAPS INTEGRATIONEvery ticket has an ‘Open in Google Maps’ button. When tapped, it opens the ATM’s exact location in the Google Maps app on the engineer’s phone — directions are ready immediately, no manual searching required.04 / Closing a TicketAfter fixing the ATM, the engineer closes the ticket by providing two required things:A written description of what was done to resolve the issue (minimum 20 characters)A photo or video as proof of the completed workThe system will not allow submission unless both are provided. Once submitted, the ticket is marked Closed and the admin’s dashboard updates immediately.— 06 — TICKET LIFECYCLEHow a Ticket Moves Through the System.Every ticket follows a clear path from creation to resolution. The status is always visible, colour-coded, and updated in real time.OPENTicket created by admin. Not yet assigned to an engineer.ASSIGNEDTicket assigned to a specific engineer. Work has not yet started.IN PROGRESSEngineer has accepted the job and is on their way or actively working.PENDINGEngineer has submitted proof. Awaiting bank verification (if bank portal is active).CLOSEDIssue fully resolved and verified. Ticket is complete.RE-RAISEDBank found the issue was not resolved. Ticket re-opened for further attention.— 07 — OPTIONAL PHASEBank Portal — Phase 2.NOTE — PHASE 2 ONLYThis feature is not included in the initial launch. It will be activated as a separate phase once the core system is live and running.Once activated, the Bank Portal gives bank representatives their own secure login. They can only see tickets related to their own bank or branch.With the Bank Portal, a bank representative can:View closed tickets for their branch, including the engineer’s resolution notes and proof photos or videosMark a ticket as Verified if they are satisfied the issue has been resolvedRe-raise an issue if the problem persists, creating a new linked ticket automaticallyRate the quality of the engineer’s work on a 1 to 5 star scale with optional written feedbackEngineer ratings are visible to the admin on each engineer’s profile, giving a clear picture of service quality over time.— 08 — ACCESS &amp; SECURITYAccess, Security &amp; Privacy.Each user only ever sees what is relevant to their role — nothing more.ROLEWHAT THEY CAN SEEWHAT THEY CAN DOAdminEverything — all tickets, all engineers, all ATMsCreate, assign, and close any ticketEngineerTheir own tickets + unassigned open ticketsClose their assigned tickets with proofBank (Phase 2)Only tickets linked to their bank or branchVerify or re-raise closed tickets, rate engineersAll logins are secured with email and password authentication.
